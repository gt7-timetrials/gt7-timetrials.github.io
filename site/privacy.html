Privacy

GT7 Time Trial Tracker: the website and the Android app. Last updated 31 October 2026.

In short: the website and the app collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

The Android app

  • The app shows this website and adds recording on the phone.
  • To record, it talks to your PlayStation on your home Wi-Fi network, the same way the free computer recorder does. It doesn't touch your PlayStation Network account and doesn't change anything on the console.
  • A recording stays in the phone's memory while the app is open, and is compared with the reference lap on the phone. It isn't sent anywhere. It's gone when you close the app, unless you tap Save to Downloads, which saves it as a file in your phone's Downloads folder.
  • When you upload a recording to compare, the file is read on the phone. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • The app uses internet and network access to load the website and to reach your PlayStation. While it records, it shows a "Recording your laps" notification and keeps running if you lock the phone or switch apps, so a lap isn't lost; this stops when you stop recording. It asks for nothing else: no location, contacts, camera or microphone.

The website

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't have access to them.
  • The website uses no cookies and no analytics. Comparisons run in your browser; uploaded recordings aren't sent anywhere.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will be updated first. Questions: open an issue on GitHub, or use the email address on the Google Play listing.
